\# Schedula – Project Overview



\## Introduction



Schedula is a healthcare appointment scheduling platform that helps patients and doctors manage appointments in a simple and organized way.



The main idea behind the platform is to reduce the hassle of booking appointments manually through phone calls or physical visits. Patients can easily find doctors, check available time slots, and schedule appointments online. Doctors can manage their availability and keep track of upcoming appointments from a single platform.



This project aims to create a smooth and user-friendly experience for both patients and healthcare professionals.



\---



\## Problem Statement



In many healthcare systems, appointment scheduling is still handled manually. This can lead to:



\* Difficulty finding available doctors

\* Scheduling conflicts and double bookings

\* Long waiting times

\* Missed appointments

\* Lack of proper communication between patients and doctors



Schedula aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized appointment management system.



\---



\## User Roles



\### Patient



Patients are the primary users of the platform and can:



\* Register and create an account

\* Log in securely

\* Browse available doctors

\* View doctor details and specialization

\* Check available appointment slots

\* Book appointments

\* Reschedule appointments if required

\* Cancel appointments

\* Receive notifications and appointment reminders



\---



\### Doctor



Doctors can use the platform to manage their schedules and appointments.



Doctors can:



\* Register on the platform

\* Create and update their profile

\* Add professional details and specialization

\* Set their availability

\* View booked appointments

\* Manage their schedule efficiently

\* Receive appointment-related notifications



\---



\### Admin (Future Scope)



An administrator may be introduced in future versions of the platform to:



\* Manage users

\* Monitor platform activities

\* Handle reports and system management

\* Maintain overall platform operations



\---



\## Main Workflow



\### Patient Workflow



1\. Patient registers or logs in.

2\. Patient searches for doctors.

3\. Patient views doctor profiles and availability.

4\. Patient selects an available slot.

5\. Appointment is booked successfully.

6\. Notification is sent to the patient and doctor.

7\. Patient can later reschedule or cancel the appointment if needed.



\---



\### Doctor Workflow



1\. Doctor registers on the platform.

2\. Doctor completes profile setup.

3\. Doctor adds available consultation timings.

4\. Patients book appointments based on availability.

5\. Doctor views upcoming appointments.

6\. Doctor receives notifications regarding appointment updates.
